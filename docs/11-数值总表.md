**公式**：

```
levelScale   = 1.10^(levelId-1) × 1.0
enemies      = max(3, round((4 + floor(levelId/2)) × countMul × (1+boost×0.5)))
baseEnemyHp  = round(55 × levelScale × hpMul × (1+boost))
baseEnemyAtk = round(6 × levelScale × atkMul × (1+boost))
enemySpeed   = round(92 × (1 + (levelScale-1)×0.35) × speedMul)
expPerEnemy  = round((30 + levelId×2) × (1 + (tier-1)×0.25))
goldReward   = round((90 + levelId×22) × goldMul × (1+boost×0.8))
reward.coin  = round(60 × levelScale × goldMul × (1+boost×0.8))
reward.gem   = levelId % 5 === 0 ? 5 + floor(levelId/5) : 0
reward.atk   = round(6 × levelScale)
reward.hp    = round(15 × levelScale)
batchSize    = max(2, ceil(enemies/3))
```

**噩梦三怪比例**：`elite 0.08` · `swift 0.12` · `charger 0.05`（普通/困难强制 0）
